About

A town is its people.

Crawley is often described by what passes through it: the airport, the A23, the London commute. Hello Crawley is about the people who stay.

What we do

We stop people in parks, parades, cafés and bus stops, ask if they'd like to chat, and take their portrait. Each story is written in the person's own words and lightly edited for length.

Why Crawley

Since becoming a New Town in 1947, Crawley has welcomed people from across Britain and the world. Fifteen neighbourhoods, more than a hundred languages, and a lot of stories that never make the news.

How to get involved

  • Share your story or nominate someone you think we should meet.
  • Volunteer as a photographer or interviewer.
  • Local business, school or group? Get in touch about collaborations.

Privacy & consent

We only publish a story once the person has seen it and agreed. You can ask us to edit or remove your story at any time. For anyone under 18 we need consent from a parent or guardian. We never publish full names, addresses or contact details unless you ask us to.
